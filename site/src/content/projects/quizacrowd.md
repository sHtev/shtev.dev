A live quiz meant to scale from a pub to a stadium: players join by QR code and answers flow through a queue to a scorer and a live leaderboard. Four Kotlin services with Redpanda, Redis and PostgreSQL, on k3s on Hetzner, deployed with Terraform and Argo CD.
