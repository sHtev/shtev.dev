**The client.** Pendragon's Perspective is an online information service for the UK pensions industry, used by pension law firms, benefit consultants, actuaries and government departments.

**What I did.** Between 2013 and 2019 I migrated Perspective from a legacy stand-alone product to a web platform, including a serverless AWS pipeline for parsing and transforming its documents. Since 2023 I've supported it from the DevOps side, and provided the cloud platform for Pendragon's AI trials: a chat assistant trained on the Perspective corpus and a RAG search add-on, integrated through Amazon Bedrock and LiteLLM.
